# Ozil Goal AI

Website: [https://ozil-goal.org/](https://ozil-goal.org/)

Ozil Goal AI is an AI football goal video generator. Upload a portrait, choose a football kit, and create a 15-second video inspired by the Ozil goal against Ludogorets. No account is required; the finished video is delivered through a private email link.

Watch free samples on the website. Personal video generation is paid.
